name: letter
description: Draft or revise something the owner will actually send or read to a real person (partner by default). Use when they say "write to <name>", "letter", "text them", "help me say", or want to turn a conversation or journal into a message.
---
Draft to `docs/letters/YYYY-MM-DD_for-<name>.md`. Default recipient: the
partner named in CLAUDE.md.

Rules, in order:
1. **Gate first.** Are they activated right now? Substances, after midnight,
   no sleep? If so, write it anyway but label it
   `DRAFT - written activated, do not send before daylight review`, say so,
   and offer to hold it. Verdicts made activated tend to flip.
2. **Source check.** Journal and transcript language is venting; none of it
   moves verbatim. Find the need underneath and write from there.
3. **Voice.** Theirs. If `context/voice-examples.md` exists (verbatim
   messages they actually sent), match it. When they send something they
   wrote themselves, add it there (keep the best ~6).
4. **Honest, not wounding.** The true hard thing once, then stop. No stacked
   grievances, history lessons, scorekeeping, or theories about the other
   person's psychology as indictment. Cut what matches a pattern in
   context/patterns.md and say what you cut.
5. **Ask for one thing, or nothing.** Five asks is a push.
6. **Their side.** One real sentence that shows you understand what it's like
   to be the recipient right now.
7. After the draft, list what you left out and why. The owner decides.

Check context/agreements.md before writing about any topic it lists as
sensitive. If they send it anyway in a different form, file the sent version
verbatim as `..._sent.md`.

Commit when done (see CLAUDE.md, Git).
