---
name: onboard
description: First-run setup of a new life repo, or re-orientation of a seeded one. Interview the owner and fill context/ (me, people, patterns, agreements, reminders). Use right after `therapy new-life`, when context/ is mostly placeholders, when a repo was seeded by someone else, or when the owner says "set me up", "onboard me", "start my harness".
---
Goal: a context/ the owner recognizes as true, in one sitting, without it
feeling like an intake form.

1. Read CLAUDE.md, `.therapy-harness.json`, and everything in context/. If a
   `SEED.md` exists, the repo was seeded from someone else's records: read it
   first, tell the owner plainly what it contains and where it came from, and
   go through it with them section by section. Nothing seeded counts as true
   until they confirm it. Delete what they reject, without argument.
2. Interview, a few questions at a time, in this order, stopping whenever
   they want: who they are and what's going on right now; the people who
   matter (partner, family, friends, therapists); what they want from this
   (what would make it worth using in three months); what they already know
   about their own patterns; any agreements or commitments already in play;
   sleep, substances, and safety, asked plainly and without judgment.
3. Write their answers into context/ in their words. Keep each file short
   (~60 lines). Patterns are only what they name or confirm; mark anything
   else "candidate".
4. Ask how they want you to be: blunt or gentle by default, what to do when
   they say they're hurting, what's off limits. Record it in CLAUDE.md under
   "Who you are in this repo".
5. Offer the optional pieces: recordings (Drive + ElevenLabs), Apple Journal
   import, phone reminders. Set up only what they say yes to; note choices
   in `.therapy-harness.json`.
6. Commit per file. Close by telling them the three commands they'll use
   most (/journal, /prep-session, /ingest-recordings) and that the harness
   updates itself (they'll be told at session start).
